# 24 Hour Party People

## Overview

24 Hour Party People is a 6-a-side football team competing in the BoohooMAN Sheffield Tuesday League at Norton Playing Fields 3G.

This repository is the team's digital hub, bringing its key information and records together in one place.

## Team Hub

The project will provide a clear record of:

- Player profiles and career statistics
- Fixtures, results, and league standings
- Team news and matchday updates
- Season records and milestones
